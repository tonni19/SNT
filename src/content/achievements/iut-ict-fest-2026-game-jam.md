A little boy stomps on ants for fun until they swarm his feet, he panics, and gets hit by a car. He wakes up as an ant, and must survive as one: gathering food, defending the colony from rival ants, and surviving a flood that finally claims his life. He wakes up in the hospital, changed, a boy who will never stomp on an ant again.

A 2D platformer about empathy, told from the smallest possible perspective, made in a week for the online round of the IUT 12th ICT Fest game jam (14 to 20 July 2026) and playable in the browser.

## How to play

- **A / D**: move
- **Space**: jump
- **Enter**: attack
- **E**: continue the dialogue
- Jumpy shrooms bounce you up to higher platforms
